The Core Problem CivicSphere Solves

Traditional complaint portals mark issues resolved, but fail to detect when the exact same pothole, leak, or drain fails again next week.

❌

The Traditional Flaw

A pothole is repaired with temporary asphalt. The complaint is closed as "Resolved". Ten days later, it breaks open again. Citizens file new tickets that are treated as brand new incidents, hiding contractor negligence.

🧠

CivicSphere Innovation

Our backend AI/Heuristic Recurrence Engine automatically correlates incoming complaints with past resolved complaints using GPS Haversine distance, normalized location matching, and semantic description overlap.

⚖️

Resolution Accountability

Administrators receive instant recurrence alerts linking new complaints to past work orders. Municipal leadership can audit whether previous repairs were substandard, enforcing strict contractor accountability.

End-to-End Workflow

Seamless coordination between citizens and municipal departments

1

Citizen Reports

Citizen submits title, description, category, photo evidence, and GPS coordinates.

2

Department Action

Admin assigns the ticket to Road, Water, Sanitation, or Drainage departments.

3

Verified Resolution

Department completes repair; admin enters resolution remarks and closes ticket.

4

Recurrence Watch

If the same issue re-appears later, CivicSphere detects the repeat failure instantly!
